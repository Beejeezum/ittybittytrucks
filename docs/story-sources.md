## Editorial choices

The homepage remains concise for someone standing beside a truck with a phone. Its Bruce section now introduces the actual owner history. The Sambar page carries the journey and color detail; Our Story explains how curiosity became a business idea. The guide and wish-list page make the next buying conversation more concrete without inventing current services.

The Sambar speaks briefly in first person. Bruce speaks for the business. Practical equipment, buying, and paperwork material stays direct. No stranger quotations, testimonials, fictional previous owners, invented use stories, or claims of professional mechanic credentials were added.

## Deliberately unresolved

- Archive results contained conflicting exact chassis identifiers and precise shipping/arrival dates. Those values were not published. Month-level chronology is the reliable level of detail available here.
- A memory summary said a hose repair was completed in August, while retrieved later conversation still discussed clamp removal. The site says Bruce researched and learned about the work, without claiming when or by whom it was completed.
- Considered Sambar and Honda Acty listings are not evidence of owned or available stock. Their mileage, equipment, price, and seller claims were not transferred into the teal truck profile.
- No specific parking-lot conversation, quoted visitor reaction, or completed Halloween event was verified. These were not invented for atmosphere.
- Original truck photographs remain unavailable to this execution session after the authorized download returned HTTP 502. The existing image is explicitly identified as an illustration. Replace it with actual photographs when accessible.
- Actual equipment, current title/registration status, road-use eligibility, mileage, sale status, and inspection condition need vehicle-specific confirmation before a sale listing.
