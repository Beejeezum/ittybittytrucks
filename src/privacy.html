Privacy policy

Your privacy,
without the mystery.

This policy explains what Itty Bitty Trucks collects through this website, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

Effective: October 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Itty Bitty Trucks is a project operated by Bruce Pinchbeck in Boca Raton, Florida. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Itty Bitty Trucks. This policy applies to ittybittytrucks.com and the forms and features on that website.

2. Information we collect

Information you choose to provide

  • Email updates: your email address and the date and purpose of your signup.
  • Truck inquiries: your email address or mobile number, your request to be contacted about finding a truck, and the date of the request.
  • Truck sightings: a photo you choose to upload. Before upload, the website converts the image to a new JPEG, which removes embedded metadata such as GPS data. Anything visibly shown in the photo remains visible.
  • Messages to us: information you include when you contact us by email.

Information collected automatically

  • A randomly generated visitor identifier stored in the ib_visit first-party cookie for seven days.
  • Page openings, the time a browser was last active, broad device category (mobile, tablet, or desktop), and the hostname of a referring website when available. We do not store the referring page path or query string.
  • Technical information used to deliver and secure the website. Our hosting provider may process IP address, browser type, device information, request details, and security logs. The website also uses IP address or the visitor identifier to create a one-way, hourly anti-abuse value; those rate-limit records are routinely deleted after roughly 48 hours.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate the website;
  • send truck and project updates when you ask to receive them;
  • reply to requests about finding a truck;
  • receive and privately review truck sighting photos;
  • understand basic site activity and which websites send visitors;
  • prevent spam, abuse, duplicate submissions, and security incidents; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

Email-update consent is separate from a truck inquiry. Asking about a truck does not add you to the email-update list.

4. Cookies and measurement

The website uses one first-party cookie, ib_visit, to recognize the same browser for up to seven days, keep submissions associated with that browser, prevent duplicate activity, and support basic visit measurement. It does not contain your email address, phone number, or photo.

You can remove or block cookies in your browser settings. If this cookie is blocked or removed, the site may issue a new identifier and some forms may need to be refreshed before they work.

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Google handles that information under its privacy policy.

5. Advertising and TikTok

We may run ads on TikTok or other platforms that link to this website. The advertising platform knows when you interact with an ad under its own privacy policy. If an ad sends you here, our basic measurement may record the referring website’s hostname, such as tiktok.com.

We do not currently use the TikTok Pixel, TikTok Events API, Advanced Matching, custom-audience customer list uploads, or another advertising tracker on this website. We do not send your form entries or uploaded photos to TikTok.

If we add advertising measurement or audience tools later, we will update this policy and add any notice, consent, or opt-out controls required before that collection begins.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We disclose information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Service providers: Netlify hosts the website, database, private photo storage, security features, and owner authentication. Google provides the web fonts described above.
  • Legal and safety needs: we may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect people, the website, or our legal rights.
  • A business change: information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of the project, subject to applicable law.

Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to requests, maintaining consent records, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations. Email-update information is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. The visitor cookie expires after seven days. Anti-abuse rate-limit records are routinely deleted after roughly 48 hours.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or remove identifying details where reasonably possible. Backup or security copies may remain for a limited period before being overwritten.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Email updates: use the unsubscribe option in a marketing email or contact us.
  • Cookies: remove or block the cookie through your browser settings.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: contact us to ask what personal information we have about you, correct it, or request deletion.
  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to know, access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. We will honor applicable requests and may need to verify your identity.

Because we do not currently sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising, there is no sale or targeted-ad sharing to opt out of on this website. We do not offer financial incentives for personal information.

Authorized agents may submit a request where permitted by law. You may appeal a decision by replying to our response and asking us to reconsider it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS connections, restricted owner access, private photo storage, request validation, rate limits, and security headers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it.

11. Other websites

The website may link to other sites, including Bruce’s website and advertising platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, advertising, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information already collected, we will provide additional notice when required.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email beejeezum@gmail.com.
