I took the long way here

Japan. Jacksonville.
Boca Raton.

I came a long way to be your neighbor. Bruce bought me in 2025, and my trip from Japan to Florida took a few months, a boat, and a fair amount of paperwork.

Now I’m Bruce’s own truck—and the starting point for Itty Bitty Trucks. If you found this page while standing next to me, you’ve already met the original.

About that color

A 2000 truck.
A 1957 idea.

Bruce’s paint brief borrowed its color inspiration from a 1957 Chevy: Tropical Turquoise, a black bumper, and white wheels. A very specific look for a very small Japanese pickup.

“Chevy tropical turquoise everywhere else.”

The person learning behind the wheel

A truck to enjoy.
A truck to learn on.

Bruce bought this Sambar partly to learn how to work on it. Finding parts, asking maintenance questions, and getting familiar with an older Japanese truck are part of the story, too.

That curiosity is what this whole site grows out of. You can start with the obvious questions. He did.
